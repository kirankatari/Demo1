# Office Wi-Fi

## Corporate and guest networks

The headquarters network name is Northstar-Corp. Visitors use Northstar-Guest. Guest Wi-Fi lasts eight hours and does not reach file shares.

## Home and hotel networks

A hotel captive portal must be completed in a browser before Northstar Connect can sign in. If the hotel portal keeps reappearing, forget that Wi-Fi network and use a phone hotspot for the meeting. Office Wi-Fi status does not describe a home network.
