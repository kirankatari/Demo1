# Laptops and the Northstar Connect client

## Reinstall policy

Reinstall Northstar Connect only after a meeting, or when service status is operational and the Backup Gateway profile has also failed. The package is in the software catalog under Endpoint. A local administrator install is required.

Borrow a loaner laptop from the service desk if the reinstall would run during a customer meeting.

## Disk encryption

Northstar laptops ship with disk encryption on. A password reset does not turn encryption off. Do not disable encryption as a troubleshooting step.
