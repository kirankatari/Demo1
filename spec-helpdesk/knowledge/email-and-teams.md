# Email and Teams

## Outlook will not send

Check the email service status first. If email is operational, start Outlook in cached mode and remove messages larger than 25 MB from the outbox. Northstar mail rejects attachments over 25 MB. Send a file-share link instead.

## Teams meeting audio

When other people cannot hear you, select the headset in Teams device settings, then leave and rejoin the meeting. A VPN drop freezes Teams media even when email is healthy. Follow the Northstar Connect VPN article if the tunnel is bouncing. Teams itself can be operational during a VPN problem.
