# VPN remote access policy

Document-ID: DOC-VPN-001

## Who may use the VPN

Northstar Technologies employees and contractors with an active directory account may use the company VPN while working remotely. The only supported client is GlobalProtect. Personal VPN products are not allowed on a Northstar laptop because they hide the device from endpoint checks.

## Split tunnel and prohibited use

The VPN is required for internal tools, the finance share, and the HR system. General web browsing can stay off the tunnel. Do not send family streaming traffic through the Northstar VPN. Do not share the portal address or a one-time password with anyone, including IT. Support staff will never ask for your password.

## Access requests

A new VPN entitlement is a software and access request, not an outage ticket. File that request through the help desk after your manager approves it. Urgent outage troubleshooting is covered in the VPN troubleshooting document, not in this policy.
