# Corporate Wi-Fi

Document-ID: DOC-WIFI-001

## Office network

In a Northstar office, connect to the SSID `Northstar-Corp`. Sign in with your directory account. The guest network `Northstar-Guest` does not reach intranet sites or the finance share. Guest Wi-Fi is not a substitute for the VPN when you are at home.

## Cannot join

Forget the network, reboot the laptop, and join `Northstar-Corp` again. If the certificate warning appears, stop and contact the help desk. Do not accept a certificate whose name is not `wifi.northstar.example`. Home router problems are outside this office procedure; use the VPN troubleshooting document when the failure is on your home connection.
