# Password and MFA resets

Document-ID: DOC-PWD-001

## Self-service password reset

Use the Northstar sign-in page and choose "Can't access your account" to reset a forgotten password. You need the phone number already registered for multi-factor authentication. IT cannot see or set a password for you. A reset does not require the VPN.

## Lost MFA device

If you replaced your phone and cannot receive the MFA prompt, call the help desk from a known number and ask for an MFA device reset. Have your employee id ready. The analyst will not accept a password over chat. After the reset, register the new phone before you try the VPN client.

## Locked account

Five bad passwords lock the account for fifteen minutes. Wait, or use the self-service unlock link. A lockout is an identity issue, not a VPN gateway issue. Do not reinstall GlobalProtect for a password failure.
