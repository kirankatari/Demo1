# Ticket priority and SLA

Document-ID: DOC-TKT-001

## Priority meanings

Urgent means Northstar business is stopped, or an employee has a client presentation or executive meeting within one hour and the failed service blocks that meeting. Urgent tickets are the only ones with a fifteen-minute first-response target during business hours. High means a single employee cannot work but has a workaround. Medium means a workaround exists and the meeting is not imminent. Low means a question or a request with no deadline.

## How a ticket is opened

The help desk may draft a ticket from a chat. The draft includes a summary, a priority, and the service name. The ticket is not created until the employee confirms. Changing priority later also waits for confirmation. Do not open an urgent ticket for a software catalog request that can wait until the next business day.

## What to put in the description

Write the service, the time the problem started, what you already tried, and whether a customer-facing meeting is affected. Do not put passwords, MFA codes, or API keys in the description.
