# Laptops and endpoints

Document-ID: DOC-END-001

## BitLocker and disk

If the laptop asks for a BitLocker key, stop and contact the help desk with the first eight characters of the recovery prompt. Do not search for a key online. The analyst reads the key from the endpoint record after confirming your employee id.

## Slow or crashing laptop

Save your work, restart once, and make sure disk encryption and the endpoint agent are running. A blue screen during a client presentation should be followed by joining from another device. The help desk can draft a hardware ticket after you confirm. Reimaging a laptop takes a business day and is not the first step for a VPN disconnect.

## Lost laptop

Report a lost or stolen laptop immediately so the help desk can stage a remote wipe. Confirm the wipe ticket before the wipe is queued. A remote wipe is not used for a software glitch.
