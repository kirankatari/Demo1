# How to prioritize a support ticket

P1 means a customer-facing presentation, payroll, or identity outage is happening now and there is no workaround.

P2 means a workaround exists, but a scheduled customer meeting is at risk today.

P3 means one employee is affected, a workaround is in place, or the issue can wait until the next business day.

P4 means a question, a catalog request, or a documentation update.

Every new ticket and every change to priority or status waits for explicit confirmation from the requester. A chat message that says yes is not confirmation. Confirmation is the on-screen Create ticket or Apply change action.

Include the service name, what was already tried, and whether a meeting is at risk. Do not invent an incident number.
