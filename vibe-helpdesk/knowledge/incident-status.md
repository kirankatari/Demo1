# Reading service status

Status values are operational, degraded, outage, and maintenance.

Degraded means some people are affected and a workaround should be used. Outage means the service is unavailable. Maintenance means a change window is open and brief drops are expected.

Northstar Connect, email, Teams, single sign-on, office Wi-Fi, and the software catalog are separate entries. A degraded VPN does not by itself mean email is down. Quote the status summary you were given. Do not invent an incident number.
